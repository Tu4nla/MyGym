# AI Portrait Source Note

Core-character portraits in `wiki-ai-*.json` are cropped from AI-generated cinematic wiki renders created for this universe. They are concept portraits, not photographs of real people.

## Rules
- Vietnamese characters should read as contemporary Vietnamese/Southeast Asian in face, hair, body proportions and clothing.
- Thai characters such as Somchai/Channarong should retain Thai/Southeast Asian visual identity without costume stereotypes.
- Timeline changes matter: student-era characters must look younger than their 2019–2020 versions.
- These concept portraits may be replaced by higher-resolution individual renders later; prose canon remains authoritative.
